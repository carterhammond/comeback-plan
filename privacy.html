Life Dashboard — Privacy

Life Dashboard is a personal, single-user application operated by Carter Hammond for his own use. It reads his own WHOOP data (recovery, sleep, cycles, workouts, profile) via the WHOOP API with his consent.

No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone. Data is stored on the operator's own devices and private repositories, encrypted where published. No other person's data is collected. To revoke access, remove the app in WHOOP's settings.
